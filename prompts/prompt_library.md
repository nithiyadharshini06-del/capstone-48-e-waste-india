## Prompt A — Research and verification

Act as a research assistant for my college VAC capstone project, “AI Infographic Set on E-Waste in India.”

Research verified facts about Indian e-waste generation, environmental risks, and responsible recycling.
1. Prioritize CPCB, Government of India, peer-reviewed research, WHO, and UN/ITU publications.
2. Provide at least 8 candidate factual claims with original source URL, publication date, data year, units, and relevant page/table.
3. Distinguish measured data from estimates and recommendations.
4. Explain the scope and definition of every statistic.
5. Flag conflicting figures, outdated data, missing evidence, and unsupported claims.
6. Never invent citations or claim a source has been checked when it has not.
7. Recommend facts suitable for three student-friendly infographics.
Return a table. Mark a claim “Verified” only after examining its original source.

## Prompt B — Infographic design brief

Act as a professional infographic designer and science communicator.

Create a design brief for three coordinated portrait infographics about e-waste in India:
1. The E-Waste Challenge — verified statistics and a comparison chart.
2. Why E-Waste Matters — environmental and health risks.
3. What Students Can Do — practical responsible disposal and recycling steps.

Audience: Indian college students. Style: modern, minimal, educational, accessible, professional. Palette: forest green, teal, white, charcoal. Use clear headings, short text, meaningful icons, accessible contrast, and source footers.
Use only facts and sources I provide. Do not invent statistics, references, logos, or certifications. Leave a clear source footer. Flag missing facts instead of guessing.
Return layout, exact text, chart specification, visual suggestions, and source-footnote placement for each.

## Prompt C — Captions and alt text

Using my three fact-checked e-waste infographics, write for each:
1. A concise educational social media caption and call to action.
2. Accurate accessible alt text describing the actual graphic, important visual elements, and key data.
3. A short source attribution.
4. Claims still requiring verification.
Do not add unsupported statistics. Describe charts accurately and do not rely on colour alone to convey meaning.

## Prompt D — Before/after analysis

Compare the initial AI-generated draft of my e-waste infographic with the corrected final version.
Create a table with: issue, original content, evidence/source, correction, reason, impact.
Check fabricated statistics, missing citations, incorrect units, misleading comparisons, accessibility, visual hierarchy, excessive text, and unsupported environmental/health claims.
Only report changes supported by actual before and after versions. End with how human verification improved accuracy, accessibility, and design quality.
