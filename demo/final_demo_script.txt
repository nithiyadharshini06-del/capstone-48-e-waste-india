CAPSTONE 48 — Final Demo Script (about 3 minutes)

Hello, my name is S Nithiya Dharshini from AI & Data Science at Dhanalakshmi College of Engineering. My project is an AI-assisted infographic set about e-waste in India for college students.

The first infographic, “The E-Waste Challenge,” shows the official figures reported by the Government of India using CPCB estimates: 1,254,286.55 metric tonnes in FY 2023–24 and 1,397,955.59 metric tonnes in FY 2024–25. The comparison chart includes selected years and does not invent a missing year.

The second infographic, “Why E-Waste Matters,” explains that unsafe burning, dumping, and dismantling can harm people and the environment. It contrasts these risks with safe separation, authorised collection, and responsible material recovery.

The third infographic, “What Students Can Do,” gives five practical steps: repair and reuse devices, keep e-waste separate, protect personal data, use authorised channels, and choose durable devices.

I used AI to help plan the research, draft prompts and captions, and structure the visuals. I checked the headline figures against original PIB and ITU sources and recorded the evidence in a verification register. One important limitation is that India’s 70.71% figure is a combined collection, dismantling, recycling and disposal metric, not a pure recycling rate. The global 22.3% figure has a different definition, so I do not compare them directly.

The final project includes three infographics, alt text, source list, prompt library, verification report, before-and-after analysis, and a presentation deck. I will now share the project repository and college Drive link after uploading the files. Thank you.
